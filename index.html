About Todd

I'm Todd Sean Mallett, an independent Canadian author and creator. I came to writing from a lifetime of ideas rather than through a traditional literary path, and I have very little interest in creating the same thing twice.

My work ranges from adult science fiction to children's books, rhyming stories, music and interactive ideas. What connects it all is curiosity — taking an idea that probably shouldn't work, pushing it farther than expected and seeing where it goes.

The 6 of Diamonds and the 64th Resonance explores artificial intelligence, technology, history and what might happen when human and machine intelligence grow together rather than simply compete.

My children's work takes a very different route: absurd characters, relentless rhymes, humour and music designed to make reading feel less like an assignment and more like something kids actually want to participate in.

This site is where all of those things live together — the books, the music, the experiments and whatever strange idea comes next.
